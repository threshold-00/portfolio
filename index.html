Frequently asked questions

What do you do?

I bring research, design and testing to complex industries, so the products people rely on actually work for them. That covers finding out what users need, designing the product, and testing it before and after it ships.

Who do you work with?

Nine years across fintech, government, social innovation and global consumer brands, including NAB, Afterpay, the Victorian Government and Athena Home Loans. I also run my own venture, RealDays.

How does working with you start?

With a 15 minute call. Tell me what you’re building and where it’s stuck, and we’ll work out whether I’m the right fit. If you’d rather write it down, email me instead.

Do you use AI in your work?

Yes, where it makes the work better. I’ve built AI workflows that take a project from strategy to wireframes to UI, and I design products that use AI. See the Afterpay B2B site and RealDays case studies.

Can you work remotely?

Remote first. I’m based in Melbourne, so I work in the APAC time zone, and I’m happy to adjust meetings to suit you.

Can you work with early-stage startups?

Yes. I’ve set up research and design from scratch, and I’m a founder myself, so I know what it’s like to move fast with a small team.

Are you available right now?

Yes, I’m taking on new work. Book a call and we’ll talk about timing.
