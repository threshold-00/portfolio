The problem

Afterpay’s global team had no way to keep testing and improving their B2B website, or its most important page: the form businesses use to apply.

How I solved it

  1. 01Set up a CRO playbook with the client, defining who owns design, testing and sign-off.
  2. 02Built a master test tracker that folded in UX audit findings, and won over a hesitant team by framing the programme around learning, not being right.
  3. 03Turned metrics from Afterpay’s data scientists into test recommendations.
  4. 04Designed wireframes, briefs and dev notes, then worked with the UI designer to apply the brand across US, UK and AU.

Outcome

Phase 1 launched with over 10 tests, combining new designs with multivariate tests.

Inside the work

What I learned

Some metrics the client wanted to test, like scroll depth, are too subjective for A/B testing. I pushed for usability testing in phase 2.
