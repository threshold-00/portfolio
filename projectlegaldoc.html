Legal Document Review Tool

Skills

The problem

Trust deed reviews were slow, repetitive work for NAB’s paralegals. The bank wanted to speed them up with AI without disrupting how paralegals already worked.

How I solved it

  1. 01Designed a first pass, then ran a workshop with 5 paralegals to validate the content and journey.
  2. 02Interviewed 6 paralegals on the updated design to settle a stakeholder dispute: how much clause detail should the tool show?
  3. 03Found paralegals always want to check some clauses themselves, like Universal Powers. So instead of a costly, fully transparent build, each clause is flagged Passed, Missing or Review needed.
  4. 04Ran regular playbacks with the GenAI team to keep design and research aligned.

Outcome

Approved for a pilot with 10 paralegals and the foundation for the scaled build. Average review time dropped from 45 minutes to 5, and the work was covered in the AFR .

Inside the work

What I learned

Paralegals trusted AI most when they could still check the detail. Their own words from interviews settled disagreements between sponsors and executives.
