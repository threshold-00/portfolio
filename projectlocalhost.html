The customer
Independent farmers and growers wanting fairer margins and more control over sales
Team
Solo designer and researcher
Skills
  • Product design (end-to-end)

The problem

Australia’s farmers lose margin to the major supermarkets and middlemen, with few ways to sell directly to customers.

How I solved it

  1. 01Defined a two-sided model: an app for customers to buy direct, and a dashboard for farmers to manage produce.
  2. 02Spoke with farmers about adoption barriers, pricing and their current workflows.
  3. 03Prototyped the farmer dashboard to manage harvest cycles, inventory and orders.

Outcome

Farmers showed strong interest but raised concerns about logistics and steady demand, pointing to where the business model needs work next.

Inside the work

What I learned

The concept has emotional pull and social value, but needs deeper testing to prove it can run day to day.
