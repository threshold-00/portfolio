Case study · NAB

NAB Stablecoin Service Blueprint

A service blueprint showing exactly how NAB would issue and safekeep a stablecoin, and where the gaps were.

The customer
Institutional and corporate customers already in crypto, who need a trusted local-currency stablecoin and secure custody
Team
Risk and business analyst, executive and me
Skills
  • Mapping
  • Stakeholder management
  • Workshop facilitation

The problem

NAB had no shared view of how issuing and safekeeping a stablecoin would work end to end, or of the people and systems needed to support it. That made stakeholder conversations hard.

How I solved it

  1. 01Ran ongoing process-mapping workshops with the product managers overseeing the build, across Wealth, Risk and Business Strategy.
  2. 02Mapped start-to-end flows for creating, destroying and receiving stablecoin.
  3. 03Marked risks, ownership, customer touchpoints and system dependencies.
  4. 04Worked through our executive to find out who held the most knowledge.

Outcome

The blueprint aligned teams on confirmed steps and gaps, gave executives and partners a clear story, and showed where more risk controls were needed. The program was piloted, then not scaled after the business reprioritised.

Inside the work

What I learned

Finding who had the best knowledge was the hardest part. Going straight to our executive, who knew who knew what, saved weeks.
