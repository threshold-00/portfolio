Threshold, a booking engine for Salty Cowboy

How a horse rescue turned manual booking approvals into a system that only offers rides that fit.

The customer
Riders booking with Salty Cowboy, and Simone, who runs it
Team
Just me: I designed, built and shipped it
Skills
  • Product design (end-to-end)
  • Discovery
  • Built with AI
  • Localisation

The problem

Every booking arrived as a WhatsApp message. Simone screened each rider by hand, in three languages, and every mismatch caught late was a slot that could sit empty.

How I solved it

  1. 01Found the real constraint: nothing on the market held bookings and horse welfare together, and Simone won’t do admin, so nothing could depend on her maintaining it.
  2. 02Ruled out a settings page and built every rule into the product itself: a rider weight cap, per-activity durations and pricing, and a three day course booked as one block.
  3. 03Asked riders for their experience and weight up front, so rides and horses that don’t suit them never appear.
  4. 04Made it trilingual (English, Indonesian and Russian), with a clean English summary Simone approves in one WhatsApp message.
  5. 05Used AI across all of it: discovery synthesis, the design system and the build.

Outcome

Simone still gives the final yes on every ride. She just no longer has to be the filter.
